# VIDEO RECORDING GUIDE — read this while recording

**Goal:** one video, 2–3 minutes (aim for 2:45), showing the live app.
**Team:** Teen Titans · GEO IMPATHON 1.0 · Challenge 4.4

---

## BEFORE YOU PRESS RECORD (do every item)

1. ☐ Open **https://flood-time-machine.pages.dev** (record the LIVE site)
2. ☐ Click **Velachery** preset
3. ☐ Click through 2005 → 2015 → 2020 → today → 2070 once (warms everything, no stutter on camera)
4. ☐ Back to **today**
5. ☐ Browser zoom 100% (press `Ctrl + 0`)
6. ☐ Close all other tabs. Turn on Windows Focus Assist (Win key → type "focus" → Focus assist → On)
7. ☐ Open `demo\video-script.md` on your PHONE (not on the recording screen)
8. ☐ Read the narration below OUT LOUD twice — slowly. Slow feels confident on video.
9. ☐ Phone on silent. Door closed. Water nearby. One person speaks, one drives the mouse (or one person does both — fine).

## RECORDING (Windows Xbox Game Bar)

- Press **`Win + G`** → click the **⏺ Record** button (or just press **`Win + Alt + R`**)
- A small "Recording" bar with a timer appears at the corner — if you don't see it, the timer is still running
- **Pressing `Win + Alt + R` starts AND stops.** Remember this combo.
- Stop after each shot if you like — each shot saves as its own clip, and you can pick the best later. (Clips land in `Videos\Captures`)
- Messed up? STOP, drink water, redo that shot. Nobody gets it in one take.

## THE SCRIPT — 5 shots, exact words

### SHOT 1 — The hook (target 0:00–0:25)
**On screen:** map on Chennai, Velachery pinned, 8.5 EXTREME visible.
**Do:** slow mouse drift over the map while speaking. No clicking.
**Say:**

> "Chennai floods. It flooded in 2005, it flooded in 2015, and it flooded again in 2020 — but until now, there was no way to ask what that means for ONE street. This is the Flood Time Machine. Type any address in Chennai, and it tells you that address's flood risk — from 2005 all the way to 2070."

### SHOT 2 — One number with receipts (0:25–1:05)
**Do:** click **2015** on the timeline. Let the map redraw. Point the mouse at the receipt line (under the timeline: "NRSC · 3,733 published polygons · 254.5 km²...").
**Say:**

> "Drag the years, and the whole city re-scores. 2015 is the year everyone remembers — this is the actual satellite flood mapping from NRSC: three thousand seven hundred thirty-three polygons, two hundred fifty-five square kilometres of water. And every year carries its receipts — which agency published it, how many polygons, and what the rain did that month. Nothing here was guessed by an AI."

### SHOT 3 — The 3D moment (1:05–1:50)
**Do:** click **2070** on the timeline → press the **3D** toggle (top right) → WAIT 3 full seconds while it tilts → drag the compass slightly to rotate.
**Say:**

> "Here is 2070 — the two-hundred-year flood corridor the city itself has already mapped. Now watch the water. That is real satellite terrain, and every block of water is that cell's modelled depth. The buildings are real footprints from OpenStreetMap. And this is not a prediction — it is the city's own corridor, stress-tested with 2015's measured rainfall. Their projection, our math, fully labelled."

### SHOT 4 — The fix and the proof (1:50–2:25)
**Do:** press **3D** OFF → click **Taramani** → press **Restore wetlands** → watch the dial fall 5.6 → 4.7. Then click **SRM Kattankulathur**. Then move the mouse to the **EXPOSED IN** panel (right side).
**Say:**

> "Now the part no other tool shows you. Taramani scores five point six. This street sits beside the Pallikaranai marsh — the wetland that used to absorb this water. Restore the wetlands, and the same street, same rain, drops to four point seven. That is the cheapest flood defence a city can buy. And our own campus, SRM — thirty-five metres up, scores one point four. The machine also tells you where NOT to worry. And on the right: in today's water sit fourteen point eight lakh residents — and over one thousand one hundred hospitals, schools, police stations and fire stations. That is what a flood actually is: infrastructure, with people on it. You can even download the full ward-by-ward table as a spreadsheet."

### SHOT 5 — The close (2:25–2:45)
**Do:** click the **"offline · no internet needed"** chip (top left) so it's visible, or just point at it.
**Say:**

> "And it runs from one folder, on a laptop, with no internet at all — because when your city floods, the cell towers go down first. Every tile, every score, every flood record ships inside the app. This is the Flood Time Machine. Teen Titans, GEO IMPATHON."

**STOP recording.** 🎬

## IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG

| Problem | Fix |
|---|---|
| `Win + G` does nothing | Settings → Gaming → Xbox Game Bar → On. Or use the pre-installed **Clipchamp** app (has a screen recorder too) |
| Recording bar won't appear | It IS recording — check `Videos\Captures` after 30 seconds |
| App feels laggy on camera | You skipped the warm-up clicks — stop, click all 5 years, start over |
| Voice too quiet | Speak slower and louder; sit closer to the mic. Audio > video quality. |
| Upload stuck at 0% | Wait — YouTube processes long files slowly on first try |
