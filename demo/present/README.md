## Before the judges arrive — the 6-point checklist

1. `start.bat` → app boots with the map, routes panel visible top-right, green/blue routes at Velachery.
2. Click Velachery → play through all five years once → back to **Today** (warms every cache).
3. Toggle **MCDM** once, switch back to **Flood**. Open the **Landslide** tab once. Close it.
4. Turn Wi-Fi **off**. Everything still works — leave it off for the demo.
5. Leave the app on **Today · Velachery · relief shelter routes**.
6. Hosted URL open on the slide laptop as fallback; backup video queued.

## The 15-second opening (memorize)

> "Type any Chennai address, slide through 20 years of flood records — and when the water actually
> comes, the map picks the safest route to shelter. The shortest route from this street wades 400
> metres of waist-deep water; our least-risk route takes 300 metres more street and wades none.
> Shortest is not safest — that's PS 1.1, proven street by street, fully offline."
