# 02 — HOW TO PRESENT

## Roles

- **Driver** — speaks the bold lines, does the clicks. Knows the click order cold.
- **Second** — watches the clock, holds the judge card, writes down every judge question.
- Everyone on the team can answer "what is YOUR part?" in one sentence. Decide who owns which
  module (data fetch, scoring, exposure, routes, 3D) before you enter.

## The 5-minute run (full version)

| Time | Stop | Clicks |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–1:00 | Hook — Velachery Today, 8.5 EXTREME | Click **Velachery** |
| 1:00–2:30 | Time machine | **Play**, let it run; land on 2015, then 2070 |
| 2:30–3:30 | The escape — routes (PS 1.1) | Routes panel: shelter route pair; slide 2015↔today |
| 3:30–4:15 | Wetlands — Taramani | Click **Taramani**, press **Restore wetlands**, 5.6 → 4.7 |
| 4:15–5:00 | Close — SRM + exposure stats | Click **SRM Kattankulathur**, point at exposure panel |

## Cuts (decide BEFORE, never on stage)

- **3-minute cut:** one line on stops 1–2, full time on the escape (routes) + wetlands.
- **2-minute cut:** open on Today + routes pair, one line each on multi-hazard tabs and wetlands,
  close on exposure. Never skip the route pair — it is the PS-1.1 headline.

## Rules of the stage

1. Never debug live. If it breaks, switch to the hosted URL; if that's down, backup video.
2. Every claim = a number on screen. Point at it.
3. Say "modelled" when it's modelled, "measured" when it's measured — the words are your credibility.
4. Don't say "AI/ML" — say "transparent arithmetic on published data". Judges reward auditability.
5. If a judge grabs the mouse: let them. Any address they tap resolves in ~10 seconds. That IS the demo.
6. Two people talk maximum. Others note questions for Q&A.

## Before you walk in (20 minutes)

1. Wi-Fi off smoke test: boot, click all three presets, MCDM tab, route pair visible, 3D works.
2. Leave app on **Today · Velachery · relief shelter routes**.
3. Browser zoom 90–100%; close every other tab; notifications off.
4. Laptop on charger. Backup video + hosted URL queued on the second device.
5. One breath. The product is finished — your only job is to show it.
